# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

ShellFrame AI wants AgentSecure Community to be useful, direct, and respectful for developers working on security-sensitive tooling.

Expected behavior:

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Keep technical criticism specific and actionable.
- Respect privacy and do not post secrets, tokens, customer data, private logs, or private endpoints.
- Report vulnerabilities privately through the process in `SECURITY.md`.

Unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, threats, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing another person or organization's private information.
- Posting real credentials or exploit details in public issues.
- Repeated off-topic or disruptive comments.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments, issues, pull requests, or accounts that violate this code of conduct.

For private concerns, contact the maintainers using the vulnerability reporting guidance in `SECURITY.md`.
